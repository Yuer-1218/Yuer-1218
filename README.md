> *"In the silence of code, I find the rhythm of poetry."*

I'm a recent **B.E. graduate of Tsinghua University (Electronic Engineering)** and now a **Visiting Student** at the **Communication Theory Lab, KAUST** — bridging the gap between **AI** and **Wireless Communications**.

- 🏛️ **Research Experience**:
    - **KAUST, CTL** (Prof. Mohamed-Slim Alouini): Visiting Student (2024.11 – Present), Underwater Optical Wireless Comm (UOWC).
    - **Tsinghua Univ.** (Prof. Zhijin Qin): Generative AI for Semantic Comm.
- 🔭 **Current Focus**: Large AI Models, Stochastic Geometry, and Edge Computing.
- 🏆 **Highlights**: 
    - **MCM 2024 Honorable Mention** (Math Modeling).
    - **China Patent** on generative semantic communication (ZL 2025 1 1141469.1).
    - Team Leader for **THUAI** & **Hardware Design Contest** (Replica of Surface Dial).
